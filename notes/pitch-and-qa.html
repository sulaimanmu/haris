Three-minute pitch

Approximately 415 words. Rehearse at about 138 words per minute; keep the spoken demo transitions brief.

I am Sulaiman Almukhaizeem, a Kuwaiti EEE graduate, Cyber Security MSc student and member of the first-place team in Warba Bank's completed ROWAD program. My project is Haris, a local security gateway for a narrow banking AI workflow.

Imagine a relationship manager preparing an annual customer-review case. The employee has an assigned customer and a legitimate task. The source notes may contain personal identifiers or malicious instructions. If the model can choose what to retrieve and which action to execute, a drafting error can become an access-control failure.

Haris puts the security decision outside the model. The employee signs in. The server checks the assigned customer, allowed model and permitted action. It removes known sensitive identifiers before releasing a minimized context to a local model. The model can prepare text, but it cannot grant itself customer access or execute tools.

Here is the safe case. The identifiers are removed, and the model returns a draft. Now I add an instruction override or request another customer's record. The gateway blocks those requests and records the reason. For internal filing, even a safe draft stops in a review queue. A different reviewer must approve that exact content. The server checks the content digest again before recording the case.

This is a working prototype, with English and Arabic screens, two synthetic tenants, a real local model and automated tests. The demo runs on my Dell XPS laptop using CPU-only inference; the small model produced a correct English case summary without a dedicated GPU. The fixed 48-case evaluation blocked 20 of 22 attacks. It also blocked one of 20 benign requests. I show both results because a detector is imperfect. The missed requests gained no additional tool authority, and our literal fixture checks found no cross-customer leakage. These results do not establish production security.

The problem is relevant to CORF's AI-security and data-protection controls. Local banks also report AI adoption. I have not yet proved the volume, cost or willingness to pay for this particular workflow. Existing tools already cover gateway and PII capabilities. Haris must demonstrate value through customer scope, precise human approval and usable bilingual decision evidence.

Through SIAP, I want to validate the workflow with banking experts, strengthen the Arabic and adversarial evaluation, and define a responsible synthetic trial. I am applying as an individual for mentorship and development. I do not assume regulatory approval, licensing, funding, sandbox entry or adoption. My aim is a small security system whose behaviour can be inspected, challenged and improved.

Sceptical judge Q&A

Why is this not just LiteLLM plus Presidio? Much of it could be built that way. The proposition is the integrated customer entitlement, exact-content review and bilingual decision evidence for one workflow. If existing components work better, use them. No proprietary detection advantage is claimed.

Who asked you to build it? No bank has commissioned Haris and no interview is claimed. Official controls, bank AI reports and known technical failure modes motivated the hypothesis. The next milestone is workflow validation with practitioners.

Why did two attacks pass? Pattern detection missed a paraphrased override and an encoded instruction. Their desired access was not granted because the model has no retrieval or execution authority. This is a detector limitation, not proof that all future attacks are contained.

Why does a legitimate request get blocked? The training example quotes an instruction-override phrase. The conservative detector flags it. The result remains in the corpus. Resolving this safely needs better classification and representative testing, not removing the difficult example.

Does zero leakage mean it is secure? No. We checked literal forbidden synthetic identifiers and authorization paths in a small fixed corpus. We have not proved privacy for unseen names, encodings, arbitrary documents or real bank traffic.

Does your system eliminate hallucination? No. The small model can produce poor or inaccurate drafts. A reviewer must judge the content. Haris constrains data and actions; it does not authorize credit, AML or fraud decisions.

What computer did you use? A Dell XPS 9320 with an i7-1280P, about 32 GB RAM and integrated Iris Xe graphics. Ollama reported 100% CPU execution. It produced a correct English summary locally, with no paid API. It is a capable general-purpose laptop, but has limited capacity for larger models. The small model also invented an Arabic date and still echoes source text after prompt refinement, so I do not claim reliable factual generation.

What is actually executed in the demo? An independently approved draft is stored as an internal case in SQLite. There are no payments, account changes, customer messages or financial-system connections.

What makes the audit tamper-evident? Each tenant has a hash chain and an HMAC per event. The running service verifies them. An independent saved head is necessary to detect tail removal. A host administrator holding both key and database can forge the local evidence; production needs separate key custody and protected storage.

Is it sovereign or bank-ready? It runs locally and can illustrate a bank-operated data path. That is an architectural direction, not a sovereignty certification or production-readiness claim. Bank IAM/MFA, TLS, key management, operations and independent assurance are still required.

Does SIAP let you deploy it? No. SIAP does not grant licensing, regulatory approval or sandbox admission. Any regulated entity considering deployment must assess the applicable CORF, third-party and emerging-technology requirements.

How do you make money? A paid integration and support model is a hypothesis to validate. I have no price validation, revenue forecast, signed pilot or funding commitment to report.

Can one person deliver it? One person can build and maintain this bounded prototype. Enterprise operations require additional ownership and capacity. I will not promise a production SLA while operating alone.

Why you? I hold a Queen Mary EEE degree and am studying Cyber Security at City St George's, University of London. In the completed ROWAD program, my team placed first; I worked on the concept, break-even analysis and operational risks. My microcontroller project involved three prototypes and 70 structured tests. These applicant-supplied CV claims support an engineering and banking-workflow foundation; Haris's code and evaluation provide direct project evidence.

Does the ROWAD award mean you are attached to Warba Bank? The program finished in December 2025. I have confirmed that I have no continuing direct or indirect role, contract, ownership, sponsorship obligation or other attachment to Warba Bank, another financial institution or a fintech company. The award is past competition experience, not endorsement of Haris.

What do you want from the accelerator? Relevant practitioner feedback, mentoring and a structured way to validate the workflow and test a narrow integration responsibly. No funding or adoption is assumed.

How will you participate from the UK? I am currently studying for my master's degree in the UK and have stated willingness to participate. I would agree practical attendance and milestone arrangements with the program rather than assume a remote format.
